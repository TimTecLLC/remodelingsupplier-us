Services: repair and major-appliance installation

These are the machines we come out for. If yours is a close cousin of the list — a dual fuel range, a stacked laundry pair, a built-in microwave — mention it on the quote form and we will tell you whether it is in scope.

Refrigerators

Warm boxes, noisy compressors, ice makers, water lines, and door seals that no longer hold a close.

Washers

Leaks, failed spin, door locks, and error codes on front-load and top-load home machines.

Dryers

No heat, long cycles, drum issues, and vent-related overheating — electric and gas household dryers.

Stoves and cooktops

Burners that will not light or stay even, broken knobs, and glass-top elements that dropped a zone.

Ovens

Wall ovens and range ovens that will not preheat, hold temperature, or finish a self-clean cycle.

Dishwashers

Standing water, poor wash results, leaks at the door, and racks or pumps that have worn out.

Garbage disposals

Jams, humming motors, leaks at the sink flange, and swap-outs when the unit is finished.

Microwaves

No heat, dead displays, turntable faults, and over-the-range units that need a careful pull.

Installation, not just repair

When you already bought the replacement, we can set it: check water, gas, or 240-volt connections that belong to that appliance, level the cabinet, and run a short test cycle. Measure the opening before delivery day if you can — a photo of the model sticker helps us plan the visit.

Service area: St. Cloud, Kissimmee, Orlando, and ~100 miles from downtown Orlando

If you can drive to downtown Orlando in a reasonable trip — roughly a 100-mile radius — you are likely inside the coverage map. We treat Orlando, Kissimmee, and St. Cloud as the home base and take other Central Florida towns from there.

  • Primary: Orlando, Kissimmee, and St. Cloud, including nearby neighborhoods that share those ZIP codes.
  • Secondary: other Central Florida communities inside about 100 miles of downtown Orlando — for example Winter Park, Sanford, Clermont, Winter Garden, Oviedo, Poinciana, Davenport, and similar towns on that ring.
  • Not sure? Put your city or ZIP on the quote form. Distance and same-day availability are easier to judge with a real address than with a map guess.

How we describe the radius

The circle is centered on downtown Orlando, Florida — not on a hidden shop address. A home 90 miles out may still be a yes; a home just past the ring may be a no. Travel time on I-4 or the Turnpike can change a same-day window even when the miles look short.

How it works

Four short steps. No portal, no account, no surprise script.

  1. Tell us the machine and the symptom

    Name, phone, email, city or ZIP, appliance type, and a few sentences about what it is doing — or not doing.

  2. We reply with timing

    You hear back with the next open window and whether a same-day stop is realistic.

  3. A technician comes to the house

    We look at the unit in place, confirm utilities, and say what we found before we start a repair or an install.

  4. We leave it working, or we say why not

    If a part has to be ordered, you get that in plain English. If the machine is done, we can talk through installing the replacement.

Common questions

Do you reach towns besides Orlando, Kissimmee, and St. Cloud?

Yes, when they sit inside about 100 miles of downtown Orlando. That covers a large part of Central Florida. Put the city or ZIP on the form and we will confirm.

Is same-day service guaranteed?

No. Same-day happens when a route still has room and the address is reasonable for that day’s path. We would rather say “tomorrow morning” than promise a window we cannot keep.

Do you only repair, or do you install new units too?

Both. Repair is the default when the machine still has life. Installation is for replacements you already purchased or for units that are not worth another part.

What should I have ready when I request a quote?

Your name, a phone number, an email, the city or ZIP, the appliance type, and a short description. Brand and model help. Photos of the error code or the install space help even more.
