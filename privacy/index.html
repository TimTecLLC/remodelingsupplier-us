Privacy

This page describes the information you may send through remodelingsupplier.us. It is a short operational notice, not legal advice. Update it if you add analytics, ads, or a third-party form host.

What you send

The quote form collects name, phone, email, city or ZIP, appliance type, and a description of the issue. The information form collects name, a phone number or email, and a message. We use that only to reply about appliance repair or installation in the Orlando, Kissimmee, St. Cloud, and greater Central Florida service area.

How it is delivered

Until a form service is configured, the site opens your email app via mailto: using the address in js/config.js. If a Formspree endpoint is added there, Formspree’s own privacy terms also apply.

What we do not collect by default

This static site does not set marketing cookies and does not include an analytics script in the files as shipped. Hosting platforms may log IP addresses as part of normal web-server operation.

Contact

Questions about this notice can go through the information form or the email address in the site config once it is filled in.
